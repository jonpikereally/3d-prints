TurboTuner ST-300 SKÅDIS Holder

SUMMARY
SKÅDIS shelf for storing a Sonic Research TurboTuner ST-300 (full size) as it sits on a pedalboard: footswitch up and toward you, display readable from the front.

DESCRIPTION
SKÅDIS shelf for storing a Sonic Research TurboTuner ST-300 (full size) as it sits on a pedalboard: footswitch up and toward you, display readable from the front.

Each print carries an engraved serial number (JRJP-TT3-0001). The `SERIAL` setting at the top of the source controls it.

Print settings:
- Material: PETG
- Supports: tree, build plate only (already set in the 3MF)
- Orientation: as exported
- Notes: Designed on a Snapmaker U1; the 3MF opens in Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer.

Source (editable Python/OpenSCAD): https://github.com/jonpikereally/3d-prints/tree/main/prints/Skadis/TurboTuner%20ST-300%20Skadis%20Holder
