SKÅDIS shelf for storing a Fujifilm Instax Wide 300, sitting on its base with the lens facing out.

Each print carries an engraved serial number (JRJP-IW3-0001). The `SERIAL` setting at the top of the source controls it.

- Supports: tree, build plate only (already set in the 3MF)
- Orientation: as exported
- Notes: Designed on a Snapmaker U1; the 3MF opens in Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer.
